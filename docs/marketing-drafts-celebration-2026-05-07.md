## Variant B — LinkedIn post (~480 words)

**Four products are already shipping "Agents First."**

After scoring 14 named products in the Agent Readiness Reports series — running the open-source rubric at agentsfirst.dev/mcp against marketing roots, developer portals, and docs surfaces — four crossed Level 3.

Naming them:

🏆 **Vercel** — 90/100 (Level 4 — Agent-Driven). Scored on vercel.com/docs. The only Level 4 in the series so far. /llms.txt at the root, markdown content negotiation passes, MCP and SDK both surfaced in the homepage hero. Lowest variance across surfaces (90 / 70 / 20). They sell to AI builders, and they ship like AI builders.

🥈 **Cursor** — 70/100 (Level 3 — Agents First). Scored on docs.cursor.com. First product to max Agent Capabilities at 30/30: real /AGENTS.md, MCP Server Card, OAuth-with-PKCE discovery, all on the docs subdomain. The agent-native IDE company shipping the docs surface to match.

🥈 **Browserbase** — 70/100 (Level 3 — Agents First). Scored on browserbase.com itself. The rare case where the **marketing root carries the score** (browserbase.com 70, docs.browserbase.com 45). Most products have it inverted. Browserbase built the front door for agents because the product IS for agents — headless browser infrastructure for autonomous workflows. Cleanest expression of "they live this" in the series.

🥈 **Notion** — 65/100 (Level 3 — Agents First). Scored on developers.notion.com. **The variance IS the story:** notion.com and notion.so both score 10/100 (Level 0). 55-point gap is the largest in the public series. Notion's platform team has shipped a real /AGENTS.md, a hosted MCP server, and structured docs. Notion's marketing team is one cycle behind. Cheap fix (~30 min of well-known files at the root); enormous upside.

**Two patterns visible at Level 3+:**

1. *"They live this"* — Vercel, Cursor, Browserbase. Built FOR AI builders from day one. The discovery surface matches the product mission. Lowest variance between marketing root and developer portal.

2. *"Great dev portal, weak marketing"* — Notion. Anthropic at 60 and Linear at 60 are close behind. The platform team gets it. The marketing team isn't there yet. The fix is two markdown files and a robots.txt update.

**At the floor of the same scoring:** PostHog at 5/100 (despite shipping their own MCP server). Modal at 10/100 (despite being agent-friendly in product mindset). GitHub at 35/100 (despite GA'ing the GitHub MCP Server in May). The capability is real. The signal isn't reaching the marketing root. Same Invisible Product anti-pattern, three flavors.

The full reports — rubric methodology, raw probe data, top three fixes for each — at https://agentsfirst.dev/reports/

The free scorer (open-source, runs over MCP) at https://agentsfirst.dev/mcp — `npx @capitalthought/agentsfirst-mcp` for the local version.
