---

## Philosophy: spend where money buys speed, not coverage

The thesis can spread for $0 — Josh's network + a tight HN/X coordinated launch + swyx pickup is enough. **$2,500 buys speed.** It compresses the cascade from "next 4 weeks" to "next 7 days" by:
1. Warming the swyx-adjacent audience BEFORE the Latent Space pitch lands
2. Putting a memorable physical artifact in front of 25 named amplifiers
3. Making the share card 5× more clickable so each social pass converts harder
4. Locking down the brand surface so search/typos don't leak

Cheap defensive moves win the long tail. Big sponsor-slot bets win the launch week.

## What I deliberately did NOT spend on (and why)

- **X / Twitter promoted tweets ($300-500)** — X ad delivery to "AI builder" lookalike audiences is unreliable in 2026 (post-2024 algorithm changes). The 9-tweet launch thread will stand or fall on quote-tweets from amplifiers. Save the $300 for the contingency budget.
- **Lenny's Newsletter sponsorship ($5K+)** — out of budget. The plan instead pitches a free guest post.
- **Latent Space sponsorship ($5-10K)** — out of budget. The plan instead pitches a free podcast booking.
- **Reddit r/MachineLearning promoted post** — high downvote risk on anything that smells like marketing. Better to seed organically.
- **Conference swag (t-shirts, stickers)** — diminishing returns on $500 of swag at a conference where 100+ vendors are giving things away. The $400 buys 1 concept; better to wait until there's a Capital Factory booth at AI Engineer World's Fair (sponsored by CF directly, not this budget).
- **Hacker News paid promotion** — doesn't exist. HN is organic-only.
- **Substack / Beehiiv / ConvertKit paid tier** — not yet. Newsletter capture is Week 3-4; paid tier only if list crosses 1K.

## Success metrics — was the $2,500 worth it?

Per-line ROI checks 30 days after launch:

| Line | What "worth it" looks like |
|---|---|
| AI Engineer Newsletter | ≥ 5K click-throughs to agentsfirst.dev from the sponsored placement; ≥ 50 newsletter signups attributable via UTM |
| Illustrations | OG image click-through rate on X ≥ 2.5% (the text-only OG card should baseline at ~1.0-1.5%); illustrator outputs reusable for the per-principle URL split (Month 2-3 SEO unlock) |
| Mini-zines | ≥ 8 of 25 amplifiers tweet/post about it OR send a substantive reply OR cite it in their next piece. (The plan's PR strategist called Simon Willison "single legitimacy node"; if the zine is what gets him to write 200 words, the $300 paid for itself 100×.) |
| Brand domains | ≥ 50 typo redirects in the first 30 days = the defense was real. < 10 = no signal yet, still cheap insurance. |

Track all of this through `?utm_source={line-item}&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch` on every link. The launch week dashboard becomes part of the Week 3-4 cascade content ("Here's what $2,500 of paid promotion did in 7 days…") — meta-content that itself draws engagement.
